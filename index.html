About

Emil Gene Bituin is a Customer Support Specialist with hands-on experience across e-commerce and SaaS environments, supporting customers through email, live chat, and helpdesk channels used by direct-to-consumer brands and software companies alike. His day-to-day work covers the full lifecycle of a support case — from a customer's first message about a late order or a billing question, through to an SOP-compliant resolution, documentation, and CRM record on the other side.

That includes WISMO ("Where Is My Order?") tracking, order processing, returns and RMA handling, refunds, damage claims and missing-item cases, subscription cancellations and retention, billing, Tier 1 troubleshooting for SaaS products, SLA compliance, and detailed CRM documentation — always following the SOPs each account requires.

Refund Request — Live Chat

Resolved
CustomerThis isn't what I expected. I'd like a refund, please.
AgentI'm sorry to hear it didn't work out. Could you tell me a bit more about what happened? That way I can check whether a replacement or exchange might solve it, or if a refund is the best option for you.
CustomerIt's just not the right fit for me. I'd still prefer a refund.
AgentThank you for explaining that. Before I process it, I can offer an exchange for a different item, store credit with a bonus amount, or a full refund to your original payment method. Which would you prefer?
CustomerI'll just go with the refund, thank you.
AgentOf course. I've processed your refund now. You'll see it back on your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days, and I'm sending a confirmation to your email.
